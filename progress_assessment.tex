\section*{Assessment}
The project has a bounded PyLith workflow and several working model components,
but it has not reproduced Cabaniss et al.'s reported eruption threshold or
timing. The principal goal remains an independent PyLith reproduction of the
paper's deformation, pressure, stress, and failure results, using the paper and
supplement as specifications and comparison targets. The observation workflow
uses Center BPR records for the 1998 and 2011 event windows. Separate-station
holdout analyses are outside the reproduction scope.

The evidence supports a partial software implementation, not a reproduced
eruption prediction. Center event-window pressure fits include the observed
eruption deflation and therefore diagnose the model retrospectively. The
12--14 MPa temperature-dependent eruption overpressure remains a comparison
target; the current failure criterion does not produce a stable threshold in
that range.

\section*{Implemented model and limits}
The PyLith workflow represents a 6 by 3 by 1 km pressurized ellipsoid centered
1.6 km below the seafloor, with its long axis oriented N30$^\circ$W. The
50 by 50 by 10 km domain is a project assumption because the paper does not
report the box dimensions. Four host cases are implemented: elastic,
constant-property Maxwell, temperature-dependent Maxwell, and
temperature-dependent Maxwell with a hydrothermal conductivity change.
PyLith writes displacement and stress fields; project code maps thermal
properties to the mechanical mesh and postprocesses tensile failure and
face-connected Mohr--Coulomb paths.

Several inputs remain unresolved in the written source. The Maxwell branch
fractions and relaxation times are provisional, the printed temperature law
conflicts with the stated brittle and ductile limits, and the friction-angle
notation is ambiguous. Tensile strength, pore pressure, full-depth density,
and some boundary conditions are also unspecified. The current failure screen
uses an explicit diagnostic proxy and is not a plastic fault-slip model.

The pressure calibrations use a fixed base, while the paper specifies a
Winkler foundation. The finite-spring diagnostic has not been made reliable
enough to replace that boundary: it accepts an initial residual of
$5.40\times10^3$ under a $1.00\times10^4$ tolerance, and tighter solver
settings previously failed in KSP.

\normalsize

The historical calibration uses Center BPR data for the 1998 and 2011
eruption windows. Each window contains the eruption deflation; these fits
cannot independently predict eruption timing. Eight available processed
Center archive files yielded 133,149 on-hour observations, retained by
deployment with source provenance and gaps. Their common datum and the paper's
exact channel selection remain unverified. The coarse prototype cavity mesh
has a 34.90\% geometric volume deficit relative to the specified ellipsoid,
so its computed volume change cannot support Fig. 4b. A continuous Center
pressure history, converged pressure scale, and stable joint failure
criterion are still needed.

\section*{Work sequence}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item Resolve paper ambiguities where the article, supplement, or a
        defensible independent measurement provides an answer. Mark all other
        choices as assumptions.
  \item Refine the ellipsoid mesh and domain until Center displacement and
        cavity-adjacent stress are stable. Improve the Mogi agreement and use
        exact Center coordinates for compliance sampling.
  \item Stabilize and verify the finite Winkler boundary against its analytical
        and supplementary benchmarks, then use it in pressure calibration.
  \item Build one continuous Center BPR forcing history and calculate pressure,
        volume change, stress, and failure through the reported eruption
        cycles.
  \item Refine the time integration until reservoir tensile failure and the
        connected Mohr--Coulomb path produce a stable pressure threshold. Then
        compare the independently obtained threshold with 12--14 MPa and the
        paper's event timing.
\end{enumerate}

\section*{Evidence limits}
The numerical results in this assessment are taken from the repository's
tracked run record; raw BPR archives, processed series, meshes, and simulation
fields remain local and untracked. This report refresh did not run tests,
simulations, or new calibrations. \texttt{make reproduction-status} lists
unresolved source requirements; \texttt{make reproduce} stops without claiming
a completed experiment. The source paper and
supplement are specifications, while their reported values are comparison
targets. No author code, COMSOL files, or author-generated model fields are
used.
